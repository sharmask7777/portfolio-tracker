# Concepts

> Shared domain vocabulary for this project — entities, named processes, and status concepts with project-specific meaning. Seeded with core domain vocabulary, then accretes as ce-compound captures and refreshes learnings; direct edits are fine. Glossary only, not a spec or catch-all.

## Core Entities & Holdings

### Portfolio
An investor-scoped container that aggregates investments across multiple asset classes and folios into a unified tracking and valuation boundary.
*Avoid: Account, bucket*

A Portfolio owns multiple Folios and manual assets, but does not directly own Transactions. Historical valuation snapshots are calculated and recorded at the Portfolio boundary.

### Folio
An account registered with an Asset Management Company (AMC) holding mutual fund units under a specific tax-identity profile.
*Avoid: Fund account, sub-account*

A Folio holds units of a single mutual fund scheme within a Portfolio and maintains a strictly chronological ledger of Transactions.

### Transaction
An immutable financial record representing a unit or cash modification within a Folio, such as a purchase, systematic investment plan installment, redemption, or switch.
*Avoid: Ledger entry, trade*

Every Transaction carries a date, type, unit change, amount, and resulting Running Balance.

### Running Balance
The cumulative quantity of unsold units remaining in a Folio immediately following the execution of a Transaction.
*Avoid: Current units, balance units*

The Running Balance advances with purchases and decreases with redemptions. It serves as the authoritative ground truth for chronological sequencing on dates with multiple same-day transactions.

## Ingestion & Ledger Lifecycle

### CAS (Consolidated Account Statement)
A consolidated financial statement generated by registrars or depositories representing the authoritative, cumulative transaction history of an investor's holdings from inception.
*Avoid: Statement, monthly report*

Because a CAS contains the complete transaction history since portfolio inception, ingestion uses Clean-Slate Reconstruction rather than append-only updates.

### Clean-Slate Reconstruction
An ingestion protocol where all pre-existing ledger entries and cached valuation checkpoints for a touched Folio are cleared before streaming authoritative records from a cumulative statement.
*Avoid: Purge and reload, full reset*

Clean-Slate Reconstruction ensures complete state predictability across heterogeneous deployments, preventing orphaned duplicate records or historical sign anomalies from persisting.

## Valuation & Performance

### XIRR (Extended Internal Rate of Return)
The annualized internal rate of return calculated from an irregular schedule of cash inflows, cash outflows, and terminal portfolio valuation using polynomial root-finding.
*Avoid: CAGR, absolute return*

XIRR treats purchases as capital outflows and redemptions along with terminal valuation as capital inflows. Duplicate or misplaced redemption entries artificially magnify early capital inflows, causing root-finding solvers to converge on severely distorted return figures.
