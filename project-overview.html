Why Portfolio Tracker Exists & How It Is Engineered

Mainstream Indian investment apps treat mutual funds as opaque boxes. They rarely expose true multi-fund stock overlap, struggle with the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 capital-gains bifurcation (23 July 2024), isolate family PANs into separate logins, and fail completely when offline or away from commercial clouds. Portfolio Tracker solves this with a Zero-I/O Rust financial engine as the single source of truth (SSoT), feeding both a strict React 19 Web SPA and an offline-first Expo/SQLite mobile app.

Four Blind Spots in Commercial Apps

  • •
    Hidden Stock Overlap & Arbitrage Noise: Owning 4 equity funds can silently concentrate 20%+ of net worth in the same top-5 stocks, while arbitrage funds distort true equity risk exposure.
  • •
    Statutory Tax Drift: Budget 2024 bifurcated rates on 23 July 2024 (12.5% LTCG, 20% STCG, ₹1.25L exemption, Section 50AA debt slab rules, and Schedule CG Table F Q1–Q5 accrual). Most trackers lag or misorder same-day CAS lots.
  • •
    Siloed Family PANs: Families invest across spouse, parent, and minor folios. Blending them corrupts per-PAN ₹1.25L LTCG exemptions; separating them hides household net worth.
  • •
    Cloud Lock-In & Fragile Math: Replicating tax or XIRR formulas inside React components causes silent web-vs-mobile divergence and requires live server access for simple daily NAV checks.
✅

How Portfolio Tracker Solves Them

  • •
    X-Ray Look-Through & Ex-Arbitrage Split: Unpacks mutual fund holdings into a 4-level hierarchy (AssetClass → Sector → Stock → Scheme), computes overlap, and segregates AssetCategory::Arbitrage.
  • •
    Auditable Statutory Tax Engine: Codifies Section 112A, 111A, 50AA, 55(2)(ac) grandfathering, ELSS 3-year lock-in, 4% cess, Table F Q1–Q5 accrual, and verify_itr_invariants against 33+ golden vectors.
  • •
    PAN-Scoped Family Registry: PortfolioScope (via @portfolio/core-view) toggles cleanly between household consolidated view and individual ManagedProfile PANs.
  • •
    View-Layer Purity + Offline Mobile: Static scanners block business math in UI components, ts-rs generates TypeScript DTOs from Rust, and mobile updates daily NAVs directly from api.mfapi.in into SQLite.

      Clean-Slate Reconstruction Invariant

      Indian Consolidated Account Statements (CAS) are cumulative historical snapshots, not incremental delta feeds. Appending rows on re-upload creates duplicate purchases and broken unit balances. When a CAS touches a folio (after canonical normalization of sub-accounts like 15161515 / 16), cas_ingest_repo.rs clears pre-existing transactions for that folio inside an atomic PostgreSQL transaction before re-inserting the full sequence.

      📐

      4-Tier Total-Order Sorting (sort_transactions)

      Same-day SIPs, switches, and redemptions can invert FIFO lot depletion if sorted by date alone. portfolio-core/src/holdings/lot.rs enforces a strict total order:

      1. date ascending
      2. Direction: unit additions before redemptions
      3. Intra-day balance progression (descending for redemptions; positive ascending then zero/non-positive for additions)
      4. Lexicographical id tie-breaker

      Numerical Analysis

      Hybrid Brent-Dekker XIRR Solver

      In portfolio-core/src/performance/xirr.rs, Extended Internal Rate of Return (XIRR) solves the Net Present Value equation:

      f(r) = ∑i=0..n Ci · (1 + r)−(ti − t0)/365 = 0
      Four Numerical Safeguards (xirr_safeguards_test.rs):
      • Logarithmic Exponentiation: Evaluates exp(-w * ln(1 + r)) to prevent overflow over multi-decade horizons.
      • Magnitude Normalization: Scales cash flows by max |Ci| to avoid floating-point saturation on ₹Crore portfolios.
      • 30-Day Horizon Guardrail: Returns Absolute Return when diff_days < 30 to prevent annualized blowups.
      • Damped Newton-Raphson Fallback: Multi-guess fallback (r > -0.999999) if brent_root fails to bracket. Note: DividendReinvestment maps to Money::ZERO cash flow.

      Institutional X-Ray, Arbitrage Split & Reality Check

      4-Level Look-Through Hierarchy

      build_portfolio_hierarchy decomposes mutual fund holdings into AssetClass → Sector → Stock → Scheme. Company names are canonicalized via normalize_stock_name (stripping LTD, LIMITED, CORP, INC, PVT, EQUITY, CO), and weights are allocated via distribute_basis_points (Hare-Niemeyer largest-remainder method summing to exactly 10,000 bps).

      Pairwise fund overlap is computed in calculate_fund_overlap as ∑ min(wA(c), wB(c)) across shared constituents, while compute_expense_analysis compares Direct vs Regular TER drag.

      Ex-Arbitrage Segregation

      Why Arbitrage Funds Are Segregated

      In India, arbitrage funds hold ≥65% equities for tax classification, but their equity positions are 100% hedged with futures. Mixing them into equity X-Ray creates false stock concentration alerts.

      AssetCategory::Arbitrage and compute_ex_arbitrage_summary separate hedged arbitrage capital (arbitrage_value) from true directional equity exposure (ex_arbitrage_value), surfaced in both Web (XRayView.tsx) and Mobile (exposures_ex_arb_json).
