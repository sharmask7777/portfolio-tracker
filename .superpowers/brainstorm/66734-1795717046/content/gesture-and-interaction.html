Gesture Mechanics for Zoom & Scrub

How one-finger and two-finger touch gestures should behave when interacting with the chart

A

One-Finger Scrub + Two-Finger Pinch & Pan

One finger anywhere on the chart: Immediately scrubs through checkpoints and locks vertical hairline to probe date & amounts.
Two fingers: Pinch in/out smoothly zooms the time window (up to 5x magnification, down to 7-day window); two-finger drag pans horizontally.
When zoomed: An animated "Reset Zoom" button appears next to the range bar to immediately return to 100%.

B

One-Finger Pan When Zoomed + Tap/Hold to Probe

Normal swipe: When zoomed in, swiping with one finger pans across the timeline horizontally.
Press and hold (or tap): Activates the scrub hairline and locks inspection HUD onto that date until finger lifts.
Pinch: Two-finger pinch zooms in and out.
