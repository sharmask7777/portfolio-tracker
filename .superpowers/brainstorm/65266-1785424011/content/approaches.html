How should the transaction view open?

When you click a mutual fund in the portfolio table, we need to show its transactions.

A

Expandable Row

Clicking the row expands it inline to show a mini-table below the fund details. Keeps you in the context of the main table.

B

Side Panel (Recommended)

Opens a drawer on the right side of the screen. Gives plenty of vertical space for long transaction histories and keeps the main table visible.

C

Pop-up Modal

Opens a focused modal dialog in the center of the screen. Good for deep focus, but obscures the rest of the portfolio.
